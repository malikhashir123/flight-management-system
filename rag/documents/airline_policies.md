## Section 1: Fare Types & Ticket Flexibility

### 1.1 Basic Economy (Fare Code: BASIC_ECONOMY)
- **Refundability**: Strictly non-refundable for voluntary customer cancellations. In the event of a voluntary cancellation by the passenger, the refund amount is $0.00.
- **Changes**: Voluntary date, time, or route modifications are not permitted under any circumstances.
- **Seat Selection**: Advance seat selection is prohibited. Seats are automatically assigned at check-in or airport gate.
- **Baggage**: One personal item included. Carry-on overhead bag subject to cabin space.
- **Involuntary Exception**: If the airline initiates a schedule change of two (2) hours or more, or cancels the flight, Basic Economy restrictions are waived, and the passenger is entitled to a 100% cash refund to the original payment method.

### 1.2 Flexible Economy (Fare Code: FLEXIBLE)
- **Refundability**: Fully refundable to the original payment method upon cancellation requested at least twenty-four (24) hours prior to scheduled flight departure, minus a standard $25.00 administrative processing fee.
- **Travel Credit Option**: Passenger may choose a 100% travel credit voucher with zero administrative fee.
- **Changes**: Free date and time changes permitted up to 2 hours before departure (fare difference may apply).
- **Seat Selection**: Complimentary standard advance seat selection.

### 1.3 Business Flexible (Fare Code: BUSINESS_FLEX)
- **Refundability**: 100% fully refundable with zero cancellation fee up to one (1) hour prior to flight departure.
- **Seat Selection**: Priority premium cabin seating included.
- **Lounge Access**: Complimentary worldwide lounge access.

## Section 2: Airline Schedule Changes & Involuntary Delays

### 2.1 The Two-Hour Rule (Statutory Airline Schedule Change)
- If the airline delays, reschedules, or alters a flight's departure time by **two (2) hours (120 minutes) or more**, the modification constitutes a Major Schedule Disruption.
- Under Major Schedule Disruptions, **all fare restrictions are overridden by system policy**.
- Any passenger—regardless of whether they hold a Basic Economy or Flexible ticket—is legally entitled to choose:
  1. Full 100% cash refund to their original payment method without penalty or fees.
  2. Free rebooking on the next available flight to the same destination.
  3. A 110% Travel Credit voucher valid for 365 days.

## Section 3: Flight Cancellations & Remediation

### 3.1 Flight Cancellation by Airline
- When a flight is cancelled entirely by the airline due to weather, mechanical issues, or operational dispatch:
  - Every affected confirmed passenger is flagged as eligible for involuntary remediation.
  - Passengers may self-service their remediation:
    - **Cash Refund**: Full 100% refund of all fares and taxes paid.
    - **Travel Credit Voucher**: Issued with a 10% bonus incentive, valid for exactly 365 days from issue date.
    - **Rebooking**: Standby or confirmed seat transfer to alternate routing.

### 3.2 Travel Credit Voucher Expiration Policy
- All issued airline travel credits and vouchers expire exactly **365 days (1 calendar year)** from the date of issue.
- Unused credits after 365 days are voided and cannot be reissued or redeemed.

## Section 4: Waitlist, Standby & Promotion Priority

### 4.1 Standby Priority Algorithm
- When a flight or cabin class is fully booked, passengers may join the Standby Waitlist.
- Waitlist ranking is deterministically calculated using the following tier hierarchy:
  1. Frequent Flyer Loyalty Tier:
     - Platinum Members: 4,000 priority points.
     - Gold Members: 3,000 priority points.
     - Silver Members: 2,000 priority points.
     - Bronze Members: 1,000 priority points.
     - Standard Members: 0 priority points.
  2. Time of Waitlist Entry: FIFO (First In, First Out) tie-breaker for identical loyalty tiers.

### 4.2 Auto-Promotion & Claim Window
- When a confirmed seat is freed via passenger cancellation or inventory adjustment, the highest-priority candidate is offered the seat.
- The passenger has a strict **two-hour (2h) notification window** to claim and confirm the reservation.
- If unclaimed after 2 hours, the offer automatically expires and is reassigned to the next passenger in line.

## Section 5: Human Sign-Off & Discretionary Compensation

### 5.1 Mandated Human Approval
- AI-generated customer support answers must be held in the supervisor review queue and receive human sign-off before transmission.
- Denied boarding compensation checks (EC261 / DOT statutory payouts) must be authorized by an Operations Supervisor or Station Manager.
